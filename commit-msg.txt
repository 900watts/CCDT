feat(editor): paper area grows with content

The white paper used `display: flex` on the page wrapper with the
default `align-items: stretch`, which forced the paper to the page's
own height regardless of how much text the user wrote. Long content
overflowed past the paper's bottom and bled onto the grey backdrop
(no more white background under the text).

Fix: page wrapper is now `display: block`, and the paper uses
`margin: 0 auto` for centering (instead of relying on the flex
parent's `justify-content: center`). Paper height now grows with
content (driven by `min-height: 100%` so empty editors still fill
the page). `box-sizing: border-box` keeps padding inside the paper.

Verified: archive 173's long content now stays on a single white
card that extends down with the prose."
